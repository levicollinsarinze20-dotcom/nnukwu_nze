Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you may want to know before placing your order.

What exactly does this steering lock do?

It links the steering wheel to the seat-belt anchor point, restricting normal steering movement while the vehicle is parked. It is intended to add another physical obstacle before the car can be driven away.

Is this a replacement for my car alarm or tracker?

No. It works best as an additional layer of security alongside whatever factory or aftermarket security your vehicle already has.

Why use a physical lock when my car already has an immobiliser?

An immobiliser is hidden. A steering lock is visible. That visible barrier can discourage opportunistic theft because it shows immediately that the vehicle has extra protection.

Does it need installation, wiring or a technician?

No permanent installation is required. It is a manual mechanical device designed for everyday use.

Does it need electricity, charging, Bluetooth or an app?

No. The lock does not depend on batteries, an app or a wireless connection.

How long does it take to lock my car?

Once you are familiar with the process, it is designed to be fitted quickly enough to become part of your normal parking routine.

Will it damage my steering wheel?

The contact section is covered to help protect the steering wheel during normal use. As with any car accessory, fit and remove it carefully and correctly.

Can I use it every day?

Yes. It is intended as a practical everyday security accessory for regular parking situations.

Can I use it at home overnight?

Yes. You can use it whenever the vehicle is parked, including at home, work, public parking areas or when travelling.

Does this mean my car cannot be stolen?

No security device can responsibly promise that. The purpose of this product is to add a visible physical deterrent and make unauthorised driving more difficult.
